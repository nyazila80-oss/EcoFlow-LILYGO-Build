AUDIT19.15.10 CALLBACK-GUARD
- Keeps BLE, WiFi/WebUI, RS485 and CAN enabled.
- Keeps synchronous direct JK connect from 19.15.9.
- BLE host callbacks no longer perform remote writeValue() or local notify() directly.
- Callbacks copy at most one 247-byte ATT packet per direction into fixed static buffers.
- Main loop bridgePump() performs actual forwarding.
- Busy/oversize packets are dropped and counted instead of allocating memory or nesting BLE operations.
- No battery protection values or PowerStream CAN payloads changed.
- Static/source review only; not compiled or hardware-tested in this environment.
