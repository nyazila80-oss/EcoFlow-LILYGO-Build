AUDIT19.15.25 CALLBACK-STATE-SERIALIZE

- NimBLE host callbacks no longer mutate sAppConnected/sBmsConnected directly.
- Connection state changes are serialized in processDeferredTransitions() on the Arduino loop task.
- Disconnect pending flags still gate bridgePump()/remoteNotify immediately.
- CAN encoder now rejects headerSize != 18 before any header mutation/indexing.
- No BMS protection values, PowerStream payload definitions, RS485 protocol, SOC limits, or BLE UUIDs changed.
