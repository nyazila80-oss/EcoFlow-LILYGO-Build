AUDIT19.15.11 CALLBACK-DEFER-GUARD

Simulation follow-up to 19.15.10.

Key correction:
- Server onDisconnect callback no longer calls NimBLEDevice::startAdvertising() directly.
- Callback only sets sRestartAdvertisingPending.
- Advertising restart is executed later from jkBleProxyTick()/main loop.

Why:
19.15.10 intentionally deferred notify/write operations out of BLE callbacks, but still left one nested BLE host operation in ServerCB::onDisconnect(). This revision closes that remaining callback re-entry path.

Preserved:
- synchronous central connect (async=false)
- fixed 247-byte callback staging buffers
- write/notify bridgePump outside callbacks
- BLE heap guards and integrity check
- direct known-MAC connect, observer disabled
- RS485 128-byte loop budget
- CAN/WiFi/WebUI/heartbeat remain active

Static/simulation reviewed only; not PlatformIO-compiled or hardware-tested in this environment.
