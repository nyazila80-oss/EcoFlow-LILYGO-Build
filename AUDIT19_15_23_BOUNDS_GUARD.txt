AUDIT19.15.23 BOUNDS-GUARD

- Keeps all 19.15.22 BLE/RS485/CAN/WiFi/WebUI guards.
- sendCANMessage now requires headerSize >= 18 before reading tracker bytes [16]/[17].
- Rejects nonzero payloadSize with null payload.
- Adds a 512-byte payload cap to bound stack VLAs in the encoder. Current internal payloads are far below this cap.
- Aligns boot/BLE diagnostic version strings with 19.15.23.
