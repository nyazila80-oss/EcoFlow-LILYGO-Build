AUDIT20.4.4.3 DEEP-RACE-FAILSAFE

Changes from 20.4.4.2:
- Low-SOC guard configuration is published as one coherent atomic uint32 snapshot (enabled/stop/resume).
- Low-SOC runtime state, block request, and transition counter are atomic across loop/WebUI contexts.
- BMS stale/invalid is fail-safe at the request layer: guard requests BLOCK while enabled. Enforcement remains MONITOR_ONLY until DCL mapping is verified; no JK MOS write and no invented PowerStream DCL command.
- Low-SOC POST validates enabled strictly and retains resume > stop validation.
- Persistence reads the coherent low-SOC snapshot rather than three independently mutable fields.

Remaining formal/open items:
- Other legacy Config booleans/fields (e.g. canTxEnabled and message response flags) are still ordinary fields that may be read/written across task/core contexts. They were intentionally not broadly refactored in this patch.
- saveCoreConfig/Preferences is legacy shared persistence; no new global persistence mutex was introduced here.
- RAW logger isolation prevents its queue overflow from evicting decoder frames, but real hardware timing/heap/driver-overrun behavior still requires validation.
- No PlatformIO ESP32 build was performed in this environment.
- No hardware test was performed.
