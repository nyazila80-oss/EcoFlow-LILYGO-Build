20.4.5.3 FAILSAFE-PRIORITY

Fix:
- BMS validity/stale evaluation now has priority over the optional SOC/SOH enable switches.
- BMS stale therefore enters BMS_STALE_BLOCK even when SOC guard and SOH guard are both disabled.
- Existing fail-closed recovery remains: at least two newly validated JK status frames are required before leaving stale recovery.
- Only after validity/recovery succeeds may both disabled policy guards produce DISABLED.
