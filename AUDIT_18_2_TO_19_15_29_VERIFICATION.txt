- FC10 write ACK CRC/register/count validation retained; 0x161E read-back verification retained.
- PowerStream CAN remains 1 Mbps extended-frame path.
- BMS-validity CAN TX safety gate retained.
- Local PowerStream defaults remain upper=100%, lower=10%.
- BLE direct target A4:C1:38:09:4B:C2, NimBLE 2.5.1, max connections 2, observer disabled, MTU 247, ATT payload cap 244.
- BLE connect call is synchronous: connect(target, true, false, false).
- Callback event FIFO depth 16 and bridge queues depth 2 remain bounded/fail-closed.
- CAN RX rejects DLC>8, RTR and standard frames; validates declared length and CRC before decoding.
- CAN TX requires exactly 18-byte header and payload <=512; bounded 532-byte frame buffer.
- Current 4 MiB partition map is contiguous/non-overlapping and ends exactly at 0x400000.
