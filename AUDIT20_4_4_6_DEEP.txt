AUDIT20.4.4.6 TX-IDENTITY-SNAPSHOT

Deep findings fixed:
1) Power snapshot used two atomics without a generation guard; input/output could theoretically come from different updates. Added 32-bit seqlock.
2) CAN identity fields serialStr/chgvolt were read directly while WebUI could update them. Added coherent identity seqlock snapshot.
3) sendCANMessage mutated shared header[6] and xorCounter could be touched from decoder/main contexts. Encoder now copies the 18-byte header locally; xorCounter is atomic.
4) Sequencer state was written by decoder core and main loop. Decoder now only publishes atomic heartbeat/start request; main loop exclusively owns running/index/deadline.
5) Sequencer deadline comparison changed to wrap-safe signed delta.
6) Observed CB upper/lower values and timestamps changed to atomics for WebUI cross-core reads.

No DCL mapping invented. Low-SOC guard remains MONITOR_ONLY_UNTIL_DCL_MAPPING_VERIFIED.
No JK MOS shutdown added.
No PlatformIO/hardware build claimed.
