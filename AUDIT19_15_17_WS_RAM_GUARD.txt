AUDIT19.15.17 WS-RAM-GUARD

Changes from 19.15.16:
- WebSocket CAN/debug rings reduced from 8192 to 4096 bytes each.
  This releases 8192 bytes of static internal RAM for WiFi/NimBLE.
- Flush slice remains 1024 bytes; oldest complete lines are dropped when full.
- Removed obsolete unbounded String canLog fallback and global object.
- BLE proxy, RS485, CAN, WebUI, callback/session/race guards unchanged.
- No battery protection values or CAN payloads changed.

Simulation/model:
- 2 x 4096 rings = 8192 bytes versus 16384 previously, saving exactly 8192 static bytes.
- Ring algorithm retains one-byte gap and drops oldest whole lines before enqueue.
- With 1024-byte flush slices, a 4095-byte usable ring holds at least 4 flush slices.
- Under artificial producer overload, data loss is bounded to oldest log lines; protocol paths are unaffected.

Not compiled with PlatformIO or hardware-tested in this environment.
