20.4.5.5 WIFI+FS HARDENED
- Removed unauthenticated catch-all serveStatic("/", SPIFFS, "/").
- Added authenticated explicit direct-file routes.
- Added /fs_version.txt manifest and firmware/filesystem version check.
- Added authenticated /api/fs/status with FS status and usage.

- Existing WiFi staged recovery from 20.4.5.4 retained.
- Firmware and filesystem OTA remain separate by design. After firmware-only OTA, FS_VERSION_MISMATCH is expected until matching spiffs.bin is installed.
