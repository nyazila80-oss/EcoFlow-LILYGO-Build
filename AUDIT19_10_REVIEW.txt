AUDIT19.10 ROBUST - targeted stability revision

Changes from AUDIT19.9:
- Correct FW_VERSION to 2.3.2-AUDIT19.10-ROBUST.
- NTP startup is fully non-blocking. No delay/while loop waits for network time.
- NTP sync/retry handled by ntpTick() from loop; timeout schedules a later retry.
- BLE initialization delayed to at least 20 s after boot.
- With STA mode, BLE init additionally requires 5 s continuous WiFi connectivity.
- JK direct-connect retained; BLE scanning remains completely disabled.
- BLE connect timeout reduced from 5 s to 3 s; 15 s reconnect backoff retained.
- No changes to BMS protection thresholds, MOS controls, RS485 register semantics,
  PowerStream CAN messages, or the verified JK FFE0/FFE1 bridge protocol.

Static source review only in this environment. Hardware validation remains required.
