AUDIT19.15.26 CALLBACK-EVENT-FIFO

- Replaced cross-core volatile callback state flags with a fixed 16-entry FIFO.
- Preserves APP connect/disconnect and BMS fail/disconnect callback ordering.
- No dynamic allocation in callback event publication.
- Bridge operations pause while deferred BLE events are pending.
- Queue overflow is counted and reported later from the Arduino loop.
- No BMS protection, RS485 protocol, EcoFlow CAN payload, or SOC limit changes.
- Corrected CAN framing yield condition: now yields every 4 frames as intended, not 3 of every 4.
- Stale connected BLE client is closed and deferred before any reconnect attempt.
- FIFO overflow fails closed and resets bridge sessions instead of losing a transition.
