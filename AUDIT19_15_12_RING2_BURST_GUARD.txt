AUDIT19.15.12 RING2-BURST-GUARD

- Keeps BLE, WiFi/WebUI, RS485 and CAN enabled.
- Replaces the single staging slot in each BLE bridge direction with a fixed depth-2 ring queue.
- No dynamic allocation in BLE data callbacks.
- FIFO ordering is preserved.
- Third packet arriving before the loop drains the queue is dropped and counted; memory cannot grow.
- Additional static RAM versus 19.15.11 is approximately one 247-byte packet per direction plus small metadata (~0.5 KiB).
- Advertising restart, remote writes and local notifications remain deferred to the normal loop.
- Synchronous real-JK connect remains enabled; async connect remains disabled.
- This build has been statically inspected and host-model stress tested, but not compiled with PlatformIO or tested on ESP32 hardware in this environment.
