JK-PB V19 UART1/013 9600 read-only adapter
- Dynamic trigger: FC10 register 0x1620
- Response accepted: 300-byte 55AA payload + direct 8-byte FC10 ACK (308 total), or padded 00 + ACK + 00 form (310 total)
- Payload SUM8 and ACK CRC16 are both validated
- Device address scan starts at 0 (3 attempts), then 1..15 (2 attempts each)
- Last valid telemetry survives isolated bad polls and expires after 3000 ms
- Decoded values are committed only after sanity checks
- JK MOS writes are intentionally disabled; web MOS toggle returns HTTP 409
