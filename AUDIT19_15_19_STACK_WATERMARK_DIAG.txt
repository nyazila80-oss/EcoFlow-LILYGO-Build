AUDIT19.15.19 STACK-WATERMARK-DIAG

Changes from 19.15.18:
- Preserve CAN RX/decode task handles for diagnostics.
- Heartbeat reports uxTaskGetStackHighWaterMark() raw values for diag/CAN RX/CAN decode.
- Keep existing task stack allocations unchanged: 2048 / 3072 / 4096.
- Avoid temporary IPAddress::toString() allocation in 5-second heartbeat; print octets directly.
- No battery protection, RS485 protocol, BLE bridge semantics, or EcoFlow CAN payload changes.

Interpretation:
- stackRaw values are deliberately labelled raw because ESP32/FreeRTOS ports differ in documented units across versions. Trend and near-zero values are the primary diagnostic.
- Do not reduce stack sizes until hardware high-water measurements are collected under BLE+WiFi+RS485+CAN load.
