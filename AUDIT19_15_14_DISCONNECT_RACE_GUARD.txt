AUDIT19.15.14 DISCONNECT-RACE-GUARD

Changes from 19.15.13:
- BLE disconnect callbacks no longer null remote characteristic pointers, flush queues, restart advertising, or change the BLE state machine.
- Disconnect callbacks only publish pending flags/reasons and mark the affected connection false.
- jkBleProxyTick() serializes teardown via processDeferredDisconnects() before bridgePump().
- bridgePump() refuses to start/continue forwarding across a published disconnect boundary.
- remoteNotify() rejects notifications once a BMS disconnect is pending.
- Existing ring2 queues, session flushes, synchronous connect, heap guards, RS485 budget, CAN/WiFi/WebUI remain.

Purpose:
Close the remaining callback-vs-loop race where sRemoteChar/queues could be mutated while bridgePump() was executing a BLE operation.

Validation:
Static inspection + randomized state-machine simulation only. Not compiled with PlatformIO and not hardware-tested.
