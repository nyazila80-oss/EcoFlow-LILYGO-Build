AUDIT19.15.8 BLE-TIMING-GUARD

Simulation-driven changes from 19.15.7:
- BLE remains fully enabled.
- Direct synchronous JK connect timeout reduced 3000 -> 1500 ms.
  Reason: BMS stale threshold is 3000 ms; a 3 s synchronous BLE connect attempt can itself
  stall loop()/bmsLoopTick()/canTxSequencerTick long enough to make telemetry stale and suppress CAN TX.
- BLE reconnect backoff increased 15 -> 20 s to reduce repeated main-loop disturbances while JK BLE is unavailable.
- Heap integrity is checked immediately before every real-JK connect attempt. A failed check latches SAFE HOLD.
- Existing 19.15.7 lean NimBLE settings retained: max 2 connections, observer/scanner off, MTU 247.
- Existing bounded RS485 RX/drain, heartbeat, CAN diagnostics, WiFi/WebUI and BLE proxy retained.
- No BMS protection thresholds, persistent BMS writes, or PowerStream CAN payloads changed.

Simulation conclusion:
- 19.15.7 had a deterministic timing hazard: worst-case 3000 ms synchronous connect timeout equals the 3000 ms BMS stale window.
- 19.15.8 reduces that single blocking interval to 1500 ms, leaving ~1500 ms nominal margin before stale gating, excluding discovery/other work.
- Hardware-specific NimBLE allocation and RF coexistence still cannot be fully simulated off-target.
