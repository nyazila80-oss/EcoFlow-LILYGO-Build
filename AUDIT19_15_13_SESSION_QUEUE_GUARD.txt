AUDIT19.15.13 SESSION-QUEUE-GUARD

Purpose: prevent BLE packets from an old app/BMS connection from crossing a disconnect/reconnect boundary.

Changes from 19.15.12:
- Flush BMS->App queue when a new JK app connects.
- Flush both bridge queues when JK app disconnects.
- Flush both bridge queues when real JK BMS disconnects.
- Flush both queues again after successful real-JK subscribe, before marking bridge connected.
- Count queue flushes in Bridge-ready diagnostics.
- Ring depth remains 2; no dynamic allocation added.

Safety: no battery thresholds, RS485 protocol writes, PowerStream CAN payloads, or WiFi/WebUI behavior changed.
